Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

iklipse is a workspace for boards, cards, whiteboards and team mail. This page explains what data iklipse keeps, why, and how you can remove it. It covers the app at abdelrahman843.github.io/iklipse-global-system and the installed versions of it.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name, email address, profile picture and role in the workspace.
  • Your work: boards, lists, cards, comments, checklists, attachments, whiteboards and documents you or your team create.
  • Notifications: if you turn on phone or computer notifications, the address your browser gives us to deliver them.
  • Connected accounts: for each Gmail or Zoom account you connect, its email address, display name and the access keys Google or Zoom give us. These keys are kept on our server only and are never sent to your browser or shared with other people in your workspace.

Gmail

When you connect a Gmail account, iklipse asks Google for permission to read, write and send email for that account, plus your basic profile (name and email address). iklipse uses this only to do what you ask in the app:

  • show that account's conversations and attachments on the Mail page;
  • send, reply to and forward messages you write;
  • archive, star, mark as unread or delete conversations when you choose to;
  • if a workspace admin picks that account, send the workspace's notification emails from it.

Mail is fetched from Google when you open it. It is not stored on our servers. So that the app keeps working without an internet connection, a copy of what you have opened may be kept on your own device, and it is removed when you sign out.

Only you can see the mail of the accounts you connect. Gmail data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI models and never read by people, except when you ask us to help with a problem and give permission, when needed for security, or when the law requires it.

iklipse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Zoom

When you connect Zoom, iklipse keeps your Zoom email address, Zoom user ID and access keys, and uses them only to create Zoom meetings on your account when you ask it to.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts the database, files and server functions.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's pages.
  • Google and Zoom, only for the accounts you connect.
  • OpenAI, only for the text you send to iklipse's AI features. Gmail data is never sent to it.
  • An email delivery service sends notification emails when no Gmail account is set to send them.

We do not sell personal data or share it with anyone else.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect a Gmail or Zoom account any time from Connected accounts in the app. Its access keys are deleted and access is revoked at Google or Zoom.
  • You can also remove iklipse's access from your Google account permissions or from the Zoom App Marketplace.
  • To delete your iklipse account and its data, ask your workspace admin or contact us below.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Access keys for connected accounts are stored in tables that only the server can read, and every request is checked against the signed-in person.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes are announced in the app.
